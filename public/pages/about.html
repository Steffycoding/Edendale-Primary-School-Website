A Place of Learning, Growth & Community

Edendale Primary School is a public primary school situated in Manenberg on the Cape Flats in Cape Town, Western Cape. We offer education from Grade R through to Grade 7, providing a safe and nurturing learning environment for every child.

Our candle emblem reflects our core belief: that education is the light that guides every learner toward a brighter future. No matter the challenges our community faces, we remain steadfast in our commitment to every child in our care.

Rooted in Manenberg, Reaching Beyond

Manenberg is a vibrant community on the Cape Flats with a rich history and a resilient spirit. Like many communities in the area, it faces real socioeconomic challenges — but the people here are defined by far more than their circumstances.

Edendale Primary stands as a cornerstone of this community. We are more than a school — we are a gathering point, a support system, and a source of hope for hundreds of families.

We serve learners from households facing poverty and high unemployment, providing not just education but stability and structure.
In a community affected by gangsterism and substance abuse, our school offers a safe alternative — a place where children can simply be children and learn.
We recognise the challenges around parental involvement, and actively work to build bridges between the school and the families we serve.
We are committed to improving literacy and numeracy outcomes so our learners are prepared for high school and beyond.
